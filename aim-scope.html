Our Mission & Scope

IJMSET serves as a global forum for advancing knowledge at the intersection of science, engineering and technology. It welcomes innovative research that transcends conventional disciplinary boundaries, fosters meaningful collaboration, and transforms scientific discoveries and technological advances into impactful solutions for a rapidly evolving world.
