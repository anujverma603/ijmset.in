About the Journal

The International Journal of Multidisciplinary Science, Engineering and Technology (IJMSET) is an international peer-reviewed open access journal dedicated to promoting high-quality research across multidisciplinary areas of science, engineering, technology and related disciplines.

The journal encourages original research articles, review papers, case studies, technical notes and interdisciplinary research.

Our Mission & Scope

IJMSET serves as a global forum for advancing knowledge at the intersection of science, engineering and technology. It welcomes innovative research that transcends conventional disciplinary boundaries, fosters meaningful collaboration, and transforms scientific discoveries and technological advances into impactful solutions for a rapidly evolving world.
