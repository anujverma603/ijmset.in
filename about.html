About the Journal

International Journal of Multidisciplinary Science, Engineering and Technology (IJMSET) is an international, peer-reviewed, open-access journal dedicated to promoting high-quality research across multidisciplinary areas of science, engineering, technology and related disciplines.

Established in October 2026, the journal provides a platform for researchers, academicians, scientists, engineers and professionals to publish innovative research findings and contribute to the advancement of knowledge across diverse academic and professional fields.

IJMSET encourages the submission of original research articles, review papers, case studies and interdisciplinary research that demonstrate scientific innovation, technical advancement and practical relevance.

The journal follows a monthly publication frequency and publishes articles in an online-only format to ensure timely dissemination and wider accessibility of research contributions. The journal accepts manuscripts written in English and welcomes submissions covering a broad spectrum of multidisciplinary subjects, including science, engineering, technology and other related areas.
